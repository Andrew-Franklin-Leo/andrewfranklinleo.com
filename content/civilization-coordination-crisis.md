# Civilization Coordination Crisis

The defining constraint of the twenty-first century may no longer be computing power, scientific knowledge, or access to information. Civilization increasingly fails when institutions cannot coordinate decisions across people, organizations, markets, governments, and intelligent machines.

This research examines fragmented information, delayed feedback, conflicting incentives, institutional silos, and the coordination failures beneath visible crises.
