Five Recurring Failure Modes

Every crisis leaves the same fingerprints.

  1. Information arrives too late.
  2. Actors optimize locally rather than globally.
  3. Institutions lose operational memory.
  4. Feedback loops are too slow for system speed.
  5. No shared coordination protocol exists across organizational boundaries.
Transition

History shows that every leap in civilization expanded coordination capacity.
