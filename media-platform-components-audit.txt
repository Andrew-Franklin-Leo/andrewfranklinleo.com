2.1 SUBSCRIPTION TIERS & PAYWALL MODELS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

FORBES.COM
- Paywall type: AI-powered dynamic paywall (rolled out Nov 2025)
  - Adjusts in real-time per reader behavior
  - Lets repeat visitors see a second story before discount offer
- Digital subscription: ~$9.99/month
- Annual digital: ~$74.99/year
- Digital + Print: ~$94.99/year (first year)
- Free tier: Limited access with ads
- Subscriptions = ~9% of commercial revenue

FINANCIAL TIMES (FT.COM)
- Paywall type: AI-powered dynamic paywall (global rollout Jan 2025/2026)
  - AI selects from menu of offers per reader
  - 290% conversion rate improvement
- FT Edit app: ~$4.99/month (curated daily selection)
- Standard Digital: ~$34/month
- Premium Digital: ~$50/month (includes Lex, deeper tools)
- Print + Digital bundles
- Free registration tier (limited articles)
- Corporate / group subscriptions
- Trial offers (dynamic pricing)
- Gift articles

BLOOMBERG.COM
- Paywall type: Metered / hard paywall hybrid
- Bloomberg.com digital: ~$34.99/month or ~$290/year
- Bloomberg Terminal: ~$31,980/year (single user), ~$20,000/year (multi-seat)
- Bloomberg All Access (digital + print Businessweek)
- Bloomberg Law / Bloomberg Tax / Bloomberg Government: separate enterprise subscriptions
- Bloomberg Intelligence: bundled with Terminal or standalone
- Free tier: Limited article access

REUTERS.COM
- Paywall type: Metered paywall (launched Oct 2024)
- Digital subscription: ~$1/week introductory, standard pricing TBD
- B2B wire service licensing (Reuters News to media organizations)
- Reuters Connect (B2B content marketplace, points-based model)
- Reuters Professional (enterprise data/news platform via LSEG Workspace)
- Free tier: Some articles accessible

WALL STREET JOURNAL (WSJ.COM)
- Paywall type: Dynamic/flexible paywall (cold/warm/hot reader scoring)
- Digital: ~$38.99/month standard, $1 first-month promos
- Student: ~$4/month
- Print + Digital bundles
- WSJ+ membership perks (events, offers)
- Free Expression add-on: $5/month for existing subscribers
- Barron's: separate subscription (~$4.99/month intro)
- MarketWatch: freemium with premium tier
- Corporate / group subscriptions
- Gift subscriptions

THE INFORMATION
- Paywall type: Hard paywall (100% subscription, zero advertising)
- Young Professionals (under 30): $199/year
- Standard: $399/year (most popular)
- All-Access: $749/year (guaranteed event access + plus-one)
- Pro: $999/year (databases, org charts, deep research, surveys)
- Free tier: None for articles (some newsletter content)

POLITICO PRO
- Paywall type: Hard paywall / enterprise SaaS model
- Starting at ~$12,000+/year per seat
- Federal package
- State package
- U.S. combined package (Federal + State)
- Corporate / team licensing
- Free Politico (ad-supported general coverage)
- POLITICO Europe subscription (separate)

AXIOS PRO
- Paywall type: Hard paywall for Pro content
- Individual Pro newsletter: $599/year
- All-Access Pro bundle: $1,799 intro / $2,499 standard
- Corporate group rates (sliding scale)
- Free trial: 2 weeks
- Free tier: 40+ free newsletters (ad-supported)

THE ECONOMIST
- Paywall type: Metered / dynamic
- Digital: ~$20/month
- Print + Digital: ~$25/month
- Espresso app (included with subscription)

- Economist Intelligence Unit (EIU): separate enterprise subscription
- Student discounts
- Group / corporate subscriptions

- Gartner for Finance Leaders

- Paywall type: Free (ad-supported newsletters)
