### 5. PAIN POINT (with $ quantification)

### 7. OBSTACLE
What structural, political, institutional, or cultural barrier prevents solving this entropy? What entrenched interest benefits from the entropy persisting? What coordination failure prevents system-level response?

### 8. THREAT / WEAKNESS
