## CRITICAL SUCCESS FACTORS

### 1. The Atomic Constraint (Non-Negotiable)
Every irreversible action must be bound to a named human with legal liability. This is not a feature; it is the foundational moat. Without it, the product loses defensibility.

**Implementation:** Enforce through Frankmax protocol + HOES system + NLO-R registry.

### 2. Power Concentration Index Enforcement
PCI must be mechanically enforced (not committee-decided). When PCI > 25, capital throttles automatically. No discretion.

**Implementation:** Automatic enforcement at infrastructure level via AINEF control plane.

### 3. 24-Month AINE Mortality
No venture cell survives beyond 24 months without explicit renewal. Prevents institutional drift and founder mythology.

**Implementation:** Hard enforcement via TDES (Time, Decay & Exit System) + mandatory board decision.

### 4. Operator Competency Progression
Operators must progress through tiers (Tier 0 → Tier 5) with measurable KPIs. Capital authority only at Tier 4+.

**Implementation:** Training (Levelupmax), certification, competency assessment, tier advancement gates.

### 5. Revenue Per Entity Independence
Each of the 8 entities must have independent revenue streams and unit economics. No revenue pooling.

**Implementation:** Separate P&L per entity, separate capital allocation, separate reporting.
