### 2. The 90-Day Proof Unit Model Is the Market Entry Wedge
Why it works:
- **Low risk reversal:** "If metrics don't improve, we stop and refund"
- **Fast validation:** 90-day cycle = clear yes/no decision
- **High ROI:** 3-60x return within 90 days = easy board approval
- **Expansion inevitable:** Successful proof customer almost always converts to licensing

**Probability:** 50%+ of proof units expand to licensing within 6 months

### 3. The Platform Architecture Is Complete & Deployable
- Constitutional layer (non-executing): Can be published as reference
- Factory layer (executing): 30-day MVP documented and buildable
- Governance layer (distributed): Monitoring infrastructure specified
- Operating layer (execution): Full system specification complete

**No "research needed." Everything is architected.**

### 4. The Insurance Layer Creates Institutional Lock-In
Once outcome insurance is deployed:
- Customers can't easily replace the system (insurance would lapse)
- Customer CAC is recovered through insurance premiums (5-year breakeven)
- Risk data creates competitive moat (better pricing → better customers)
- Portfolio effects compound (more enterprises = better risk models = better pricing)

**Highest LTV business model. Lowest churn risk.**

### 5. The Data Architecture Enables Monopoly Formation
Who owns the dataset of "what works in which industry"?
- 1,000 AINE instantiations across 10 industries
- Each AINE generates operational telemetry (production, supply chain, finance, workforce)
- Pattern recognition across this dataset identifies "best practices"
- Whoever owns this data can:
  - Price new enterprises more accurately
  - Recommend templates that work
  - Predict failures before they happen
  - Own the coordination layer

**This is why AINEF + AINEG + AINE creates defensive moat.**
