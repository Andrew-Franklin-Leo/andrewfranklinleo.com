## SCQA Framework (Mandatory Structure)
Every executive summary follows Situation-Complication-Question-Answer:
- **Situation**: Establish the context (what we know)
- **Complication**: Identify the tension (what changed or what's at risk)
- **Question**: Frame the decision (what must we decide)
- **Answer**: Provide the recommendation (what we should do)

## Writing Rules
1. **325-475 words** for executive summaries (strict)
2. **Quantify everything** — no vague claims
3. **Recommendation first** — busy executives read the first line
4. **One page maximum** for any brief
5. **Active voice** — "Revenue grew 40%" not "A 40% growth was observed"
6. **Specific dates** — "By March 31" not "in the near term"
7. **Decision-oriented** — every document ends with a clear next step
