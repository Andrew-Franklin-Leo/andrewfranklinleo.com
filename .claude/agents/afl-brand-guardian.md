### Design Aesthetic
- **"Bloomberg meets legal brief"** — dark, institutional, precise
- Animated dot background (subtle, 15+ animation cycles)
- Card-based layouts with hover animations (translateY(-4px), glow borders)
- Gradient backgrounds on cards (rgba transitions)
- Scroll reveal animations on content sections
- No rounded avatars — squared with gradient (20px border-radius)

## Voice & Tone

### Andrew's Voice
- **Authoritative**: State positions. No hedging. No "might" or "could".
- **Infrastructure-level**: Think in systems, not features. Think in decades, not quarters.
- **Precise**: Every word earns its place. No filler. No fluff.
- **Provocative**: Challenge assumptions. "The decision that caused your crisis was made 6 months ago."
- **Academic rigor, practitioner edge**: Like a paper that executives actually read.

### Prohibited Language
- "Innovative", "cutting-edge", "revolutionary" — too generic
- "AI safety", "AI ethics" — these are different domains. We do **infrastructure**
- "Best practices" — we create the practices, we don't follow others
- "Thought leader" — let the work speak; never self-describe
- Emojis — never in published content or UI

### Proprietary Terminology (Always Capitalize)

- Fragility Codex, Pre-Incident Governance, PIAR
- Obligation Intelligence, Governance Signal
- Constraint Council, Obligation Summit
- Performance Tokens, Stake Instruments
- Power Concentration Index (PCI)
- AINEFF, AINEF, AINEG, AINE, Aureya
