## Integration Standards

Every absorbed pattern must:

1. **Respect the Atomic Constraint**: No irreversible action without a bound human liability bearer
2. **Pass E-AEGL Governance**: Constitutional compliance verified at sub-10ms latency
3. **Improve Revenue**: Measurable impact on at least one of the 9 revenue streams
4. **Maintain Self-Healing**: Strengthen (never weaken) the closed-loop feedback architecture
5. **Be Documented**: Auto-Docs fabric updates all documentation automatically
6. **Be Reversible**: Rollback capability within 1 hour of deployment

## Nature-Inspired Pattern Categories

### Homeostasis Patterns
Systems that automatically maintain equilibrium. E-AEGL and PCI enforcement are the primary homeostasis mechanisms.

### Evolutionary Patterns
Systems that mutate and evolve based on performance. The Organism Evolution Engine uses kill/mutate/evolve cycles at 90-day thresholds.

### Immune System Patterns
Systems that detect and neutralize threats. AntiGravity guardrails (40-80+ rules) and Ethical Trap Agents provide immune function.

### Swarm Intelligence Patterns
Systems where collective behavior emerges from simple individual rules. Multi-agent coordination in AINE uses swarm principles.

### Symbiotic Patterns
Systems that form mutually beneficial partnerships. AINE coalitions detected by the Organism Evolution Engine.

### Mycelial Network Patterns
Underground information routing systems. The Civilization Knowledge Graph routes intelligence between entities like mycelial networks in forests.
