# Monetization Strategy

Every system in the Aureya ecosystem operates as a standalone profit center with independent monetization. This document details the complete revenue architecture across all customer tiers, product lines, and revenue streams.

The monetization philosophy is simple: **specific operational problems solved with measurable financial outcomes**. Not consulting. Not training. Not advice. Proof of value within 90 days, or the engagement stops.

## Pricing Principles

1. **Value-Based Pricing** -- Price is set relative to the financial outcome delivered, not the cost of delivery. A $95K sprint that delivers $1.14M in annual savings is priced at 8.3% of value.

2. **Kill-Condition Risk Reversal** -- Every proof unit includes a kill condition. If measurable improvement does not occur by the specified week, the engagement stops. This removes buyer risk.

3. **Expansion Logic** -- Every engagement is designed to expand. Proof unit converts to licensing. Licensing converts to platform. Platform converts to insurance. Insurance creates institutional lock-in.

4. **No Discounting** -- Price represents value delivered. Discounting trains buyers to wait for discounts. Instead, scope is adjusted to match budget.

5. **Annual Contracts Preferred** -- Monthly subscriptions are available, but annual contracts receive 2 months free (equivalent to 17% discount) in exchange for commitment.

6. **Usage-Based Ceiling** -- Metered pricing has a monthly ceiling to prevent cost anxiety. Once the ceiling is reached, additional usage is free for that month.
