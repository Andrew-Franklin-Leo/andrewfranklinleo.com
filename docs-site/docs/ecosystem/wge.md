# Work Genesis Engine (WGE)

The Work Genesis Engine generates, decomposes, and orchestrates workflows across AI-native enterprises. It transforms business objectives into executable task sequences, matches capabilities to requirements, and ensures every workflow operates within governance constraints.

WGE is the bridge between strategy and execution. It takes a buyer complaint, decomposes it into measurable actions, deploys the right operators, and tracks outcomes against kill conditions.

## Entity Structure

WGE operates through a formal entity stack where each layer has clear responsibilities and constraints.

### Constitutional Layer (Non-Operating)

**AINEFF (Foundational Charter Entity)**

The non-executing constitutional body that defines mandate boundaries, authority ceilings, failure thresholds, escalation rules, and death clauses. It never runs deals. It cannot touch money.

**Purpose:** Prevent power accumulation and institutional drift.

### Factory Layer (Birth/Death Only)

**AINEF (Venture Creation and Termination Vehicle)**

Spins up new venture cells, defines their scope, installs temporary governance, and kills them when KPIs fail. It does not operate them.

**Purpose:** Venture foundry with automatic euthanasia protocol.

### Coordination Layer (Zero Intelligence)

**AINEG (Zero-Intelligence Coordination Entity)**

Purely mechanical: compliance routing, documentation flows, audit trails, human ratification scheduling, conflict detection. No judgment. No discretion. Just rule enforcement.

**Purpose:** The immune system of the enterprise.

### Execution Layer (Living Layer)

**Venture Cells (Revenue Units)**

Each cell has a specific buyer complaint, a 60-90 day proof mandate, a kill trigger, bounded capital, and 3-5 execution operators. Cells are mortal. No immortality clause. No permanent fiefdoms.

**Purpose:** Revenue origination.

### Operator Supply Layer

**Operator Guild / Talent Engine**

Certifies operators, maintains performance ledger, matches operators to venture cells, reallocates talent post-kill, enforces cooling-off and conflict rules.

**Purpose:** Compound human capital across ventures.

### Capital Interface Layer

**Capital SPV Network (Deal-Specific Vehicles)**

Each proof unit has a ring-fenced SPV with defined downside limits and pre-agreed liquidation logic. No pooled capital black box. Capital flows per deal, not per ego.

### Compliance and Audit Layer

**Frankmax Integration Entity**

Audit, legal, advisory, certification, learning -- with zero operating power. They validate, flag, enforce reporting, and trigger escalation. They never control execution.

### Human Discipline Infrastructure (HCDI)

- **Human Coordination Ledger (HCL):** Every decision leaves metadata
- **Mandatory Decision Expiry:** No eternal human decisions
- **Conflict-of-Interest Detection:** Automatic recusal triggers
- **Learning Friction:** Humans cannot aggregate power through silent coordination

### Market Interface Layer

**Buyer Interface Entity**

Complaint intake, proof scoping, budget anchoring, executive sponsor mapping. Converts narrative into bounded execution mandates.

## WGE-ECP (Entity Coordination Protocol)

WGE extends individual coordination to entity-level coordination through the Entity Coordination Protocol.

### Protocol Layers

**Layer 0: Identity Layer**
Each entity has a unique economic identity (legal + operational). No ghost units. No unregistered entities.

**Layer 1: Mandate Contract**
Every entity operates under a signed mandate defining:
- Objective vector (what must move)
- Time horizon
- Capital ceiling
- Allowed actions
- Prohibited zones
- Termination conditions

**Layer 2: State Emission**
Entities emit periodic signed state snapshots containing:
- KPI delta against targets
- Risk flags and severity
- Resource utilization
- External dependencies
- Control integrity signals

**Layer 3: Escalation Engine**
Automatic escalation triggers:
- KPI deviation exceeding threshold
- Capital burn exceeding tolerance
- Control integrity failure
- Conflict-of-interest detection

Actions: Review, freeze expansion, reduce capital flow, or terminate.

**Layer 4: Inter-Entity API**
Entities interact only through defined interfaces:
- Capital request
- Operator request
- Data exchange
- Compliance verification
- Revenue share settlement

No informal channels. All interaction passes through the protocol.

## Critical Success Factors

### Sales Language Rules

**Do say:**
- "We release cash in 90 days or we stop"
- "12x ROI on your investment"
- "Kill condition at Week 4 -- if metrics do not move, we refund"
- "Expansion to recurring licensing after proof"

**Do not say:**
- "AI training"
- "Digital transformation"
- "Change management"
- "Consulting engagement"

The positioning is **specific operational problems solved with measurable financial outcomes**, not education or advisory.

### Risk Factors

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| Slow initial BD/sales | Use financial language, not training language |
| Buyer perceives as "another training company" | Lead with cash outcomes, not learning outcomes |
| Governance complexity scares buyers | 90-day proof with kill clause reduces perceived risk |
| Competition from large consultants | Speed to market, proof-unit velocity, reference customers |
| Founder bandwidth | Separate teams for proof units vs platform |
