# Autonomous Marketplaces

The Aureya ecosystem operates five autonomous marketplaces that function as self-sustaining economic systems. Each marketplace connects supply and demand within a specific domain, generates transaction-based revenue, and operates under the constitutional constraints of AINEFF.

These are not traditional SaaS platforms with listing fees. They are autonomous economic systems where pricing, matching, quality assurance, and dispute resolution are handled by AI agents within governance bounds.

---

## Marketplace 2: Talent Marketplace

### Purpose

The Talent Marketplace certifies, matches, and deploys human operators across venture cells and AINE instances. It is not a job board. It is a performance-tracked talent engine that compounds operator capability across engagements.

### Supply Side

| Listing Type | Description | Rate Range |
|-------------|-------------|-----------|
| Certified Operators | Operators with proven sprint execution track record | $2,000-$20,000/month |
| Specialist Consultants | Domain experts for specific verticals | $5,000-$50,000/engagement |
| Sprint Team Leaders | Experienced proof unit leaders | $10,000-$30,000/month |
| Governance Auditors | Certified compliance and governance reviewers | $5,000-$15,000/audit |
| Implementation Engineers | Technical deployment specialists | $3,000-$25,000/month |

### Certification System

Operators are certified through a multi-level system based on demonstrated performance:

| Level | Requirements | Privileges | Premium |
|-------|-------------|-----------|---------|
| Level 1: Apprentice | Training completion | Basic engagements only | Base rate |
| Level 2: Practitioner | 1 successful sprint | Standard engagements | +15% rate |
| Level 3: Specialist | 3 successful sprints, 1 vertical mastery | Vertical-specific engagements | +30% rate |
| Level 4: Expert | 5+ sprints, cross-vertical experience | All engagements, team lead eligible | +50% rate |
| Level 5: Master | 10+ sprints, documented innovations | Any engagement, training delivery | +100% rate |

### Performance Ledger

Every operator maintains a transparent performance ledger:

| Metric | Tracking | Impact |
|--------|---------|--------|
| Sprint Success Rate | Percentage of sprints meeting kill conditions | Certification level |
| Client Satisfaction | Rating from sprint sponsors | Matching priority |
| Metric Improvement | Quantified improvement delivered | Rate premium |
| Specialization Depth | Vertical expertise breadth and depth | Matching scope |
| Collaboration Score | Effectiveness in team settings | Team lead eligibility |
| Compliance Record | Governance adherence | Continued certification |

### Transaction Model

## Marketplace 4: Insurance Marketplace

### Purpose

The Insurance Marketplace is where parametric AI outcome insurance is priced, sold, and managed. It connects enterprises needing risk coverage with insurance capacity providers (reinsurers, risk funds, institutional investors).

### Products

| Product | Premium Model | Payout Trigger | Coverage |
|---------|-------------|---------------|----------|
| AINE Performance Insurance | 8-15% of revenue | KPI below threshold | Revenue shortfall |
| Uptime Insurance | Fixed monthly premium | Downtime exceeding SLA | Lost productivity |
| Outcome Insurance | 10-20% of target outcome value | Outcome not achieved | Investment recovery |
| Compliance Insurance | Risk-based premium | Regulatory penalty | Fine coverage |
| Portfolio Insurance | 5-10% of portfolio revenue | Portfolio loss exceeding limit | Loss mitigation |

### Dynamic Pricing

Insurance premiums are repriced weekly based on operational telemetry:

| Signal | Premium Impact |
|--------|---------------|
| KPI improvement trend | Premium decreases 2-5% per week |
| KPI deterioration trend | Premium increases 5-10% per week |
| Compliance breach history | Premium increases 10-20% per incident |
| Agent performance improvement | Premium decreases 1-3% per cycle |
| External risk signal elevation | Premium increases 3-8% per signal |
| Tenure without claim | Premium decreases 1% per quarter |

## Marketplace Governance

### Constitutional Constraints

All marketplaces operate under AINEFF constitutional constraints:

| Constraint | Implementation |
|-----------|---------------|
| No Monopoly Formation | No single supplier may exceed 25% of marketplace volume |
| Transparent Pricing | All pricing logic is auditable |
| Fair Matching | Matching algorithms do not favor any participant |
| Dispute Resolution | Multi-step resolution with human arbitration |
| Data Sovereignty | Participant data owned by participant, not marketplace |
| Kill Switch | Any marketplace can be suspended or terminated |

### Quality Enforcement

| Mechanism | Trigger | Action |
|-----------|---------|--------|
| Performance Review | Quarterly | Review all listings against quality standards |
| Participant Rating | Per transaction | Update supplier reliability score |
| Automatic Delisting | Rating below 3.0/5.0 for 3 months | Remove from active listings |
| Fraud Suspension | Any fraud detection signal | Immediate suspension pending review |
| Compliance Audit | Semi-annual | Full compliance review of top 20% of suppliers |

### Revenue Share Model

| Party | Share | Justification |
|-------|-------|--------------|
| Supplier | 75-90% | Primary value creator |
| Marketplace (take rate) | 10-25% | Matching, quality, infrastructure |
| Governance Fund | 2-5% of take | Constitutional enforcement, dispute resolution |
| Insurance Reserve | 1-3% of take | Transaction guarantee fund |

## Cross-Marketplace Synergies

The five marketplaces create network effects that strengthen each other:

| Synergy | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Template to Talent | AINE templates purchased drive demand for operators to deploy them |
| Service to Insurance | Proof units create insurable outcomes, driving insurance demand |
| Talent to Service | Certified operators are matched to service engagements |
| Technology to Template | Tools and APIs are bundled into enterprise templates |
| Insurance to Service | Insurance requirements drive demand for governance services |

These synergies create a flywheel effect: growth in one marketplace accelerates growth in all others.
