### FEATURE CATEGORY 2: NEWSLETTER & CONTENT ARCHITECTURE

#### Best Practice: Smart Brevity (Axios Pro)
Axios Pro's $599/year newsletters work because of the "Smart Brevity" format: every piece starts with a headline, "Why it matters", and key points. Efficiency of consumption is the value prop for time-poor executives.

**→ andrewfranklinleo.com Implementation:**
- The "Obligation Intelligence" briefing adopts a structured format:
  - **`THE SIGNAL:`** — This week's most important AI governance development
  - **`WHY IT MATTERS:`** — The obligation infrastructure implications
  - **`THE PRECEDENT SET:`** — Which frameworks are implicated
  - **`WHAT OPERATORS MUST DO:`** — Concrete action or awareness point
- Weekly. 800 words max. Governance-grade quality.

#### Best Practice: Author Equity Stake (Puck News)
Puck's writers own equity + earn bonuses based on their subscriber numbers. This makes writers co-invested in audience growth. It incentivises quality and reduces churn.

**→ andrewfranklinleo.com Implementation:**
- When Guest Contributors are added to the Governance Advisory Board (Forbes Councils model), their contributions include a revenue share if their essays drive measurable subscriptions
- Guest contributor track: drives engagement + locks in prominent authors

#### Best Practice: Free Research as Consulting Pipeline (McKinsey Global Institute)
MGI publishes full-length, high-quality research reports completely free. This builds McKinsey's credibility, generates inbound consulting enquiries, and positions them as the thought leaders of management practice.

**→ andrewfranklinleo.com Implementation:**
- Publish ONE major governance research report per year (free, publicly available, deeply cited)
- Example: "The State of AI Accountability Infrastructure: 2026 Global Report"
- Positions Andrew as a *research institution* — not just a commentator
- Generates inbound for all 9 paid revenue streams
- *The free report is the top-of-funnel product. The paid access is the capture mechanism.*

#### Best Practice: Deals Tracker / Org Charts / Data Tools (The Information, Axios Pro)
Axios Pro Deals includes a database of 60,000+ transactions. The Information includes AI Org Charts and Generative AI Database. Structured data products convert well to enterprise subscriptions.

**→ andrewfranklinleo.com Implementation:**

## KEY DESIGN PRINCIPLES (Distilled from All 15 Platforms)

1. **Reader-fund first** (FT model) — Subscriptions should eventually outreplicate advertising. The audience is the asset.
2. **First-party data is the real moat** (FT, WSJ) — Registration before content access. Every email is gold.
3. **Free research creates the largest paid pipeline** (McKinsey MGI) — The annual report is not charity. It is your most powerful sales tool.
4. **Bundle > single product** (Stratechery Plus, Bloomberg) — Single subscriptions are commodities. Bundles are defensible.
5. **Analyst access justifies enterprise pricing** (Gartner) — Access to Andrew is the product, not just the content.
6. **Events are both revenue AND recruitment** (Morning Brew, FT Live) — Every attendee is a potential subscriber.
7. **Referrals are free distribution** (Morning Brew) — Build the programme before you need it.
8. **Inner Circle = intimacy at scale** (Puck) — The highest-value relationships are with your most engaged, not your most numerous.
9. **Certification creates recurring revenue + market signal** (Forbes badging) — The "Certified by Andrew Palupillai" seal is a recurring audit product and a distribution mechanism.
10. **Data tools = structural switching costs** (Bloomberg Terminal, Gartner) — When your intelligence product is embedded in a client's workflow, cancellation becomes existential.
