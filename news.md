SOURCE STATE
     +
MATERIAL CHANGE
     +
CONTEXT
     +
ACTOR / AGENCY
     +
AUTHORITY
     +
INTERVENTION
     +
EVIDENCE
     +
DESTINATION STATE
     +
UNCERTAINTY
     +
CAUSAL RELATION

Everything else can potentially be derived or attached.

Notice what is missing:

giant ontology
universal ledger
blockchain
AI-specific architecture
legal judgment
accounting standard.

Those are applications or interpretations.

The underlying object is simply a verifiable relationship between states and their material causal transformation.
